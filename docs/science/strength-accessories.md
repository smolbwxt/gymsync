# Accessories for strength — corpus verdict

Owner challenge 2026-08-21: "Can you not train strength on isolation
exercises?? Tricep work in isolation for strength is critical!" The
generator's strength filter stripped every isolation slot. Four-agent
deep-read over the 16 densest transcripts from the strength-authoritative
channels (Stronger By Science, Barbell Medicine, Juggernaut, Data Driven
Strength, Westside, RP, Garage Strength): 70 findings.
Raw: tools/youtube-research/passes/strength-accessories/f-*.json.

## Verdict: the owner is right; the discipline is dose, not exclusion

**Necessity (23 findings).** Assistance lifts strengthen core lifts via
three mechanisms (RP, strong): better hypertrophy stimulus-to-fatigue
than more heavy compound sets, targeted weak-point strengthening, and
fatigue/psychological relief. Muscle size is a NON-specific strength
driver — accessories build it at lower joint cost. Compounds
anatomically under-train specific lift-critical muscles: hamstrings
barely grow from squats (biarticular cancellation; 10-week trial),
triceps long head is shortchanged by pressing (overhead extension rated
A-tier for powerlifters — the owner's exact example), anterior core is
the classic posterior-dominant weak point (reverse hyper + hanging knee
raise pairing).

**The counterweight (12 cautions).** Specificity still rules ("nothing
improves a core lift like the lift"); accessory transfer is not
guaranteed (+90 lb dip strength → zero bench improvement); accessories
must EARN THEIR KEEP against an identified weak point; heavy multi-joint
accessories stay at RIR 2-3 / RPE 7-8 (a lifter chasing a 3-6-rep
pushdown PR injured his elbow); near a peak, accessory volume drops to
maintenance.

**Dose & placement.** Accessories ride the same session AFTER mains.
Single-joint work lives at 8-15 reps and may approach failure; rest
shorter than mains (~1.5 min vs 2-4). Accessory share periodizes down as
intensity rises (a cited 15-week cycle: 16 → 12 → 8 weekly sets with the
squat's share rising 25% → 75%).

## Generator change (shipped with this doc)

Strength day templates keep mains untouched and add up to TWO supportive
isolations from the assistance canon: lower/legs → hamstrings + core;
upper → triceps; push → triceps + shoulders; pull → back + shoulders;
full-body → core. Calves/quads/biceps/chest isolation remain hypertrophy
slots. The session-cap ladder still trims accessories first when time is
short, and block-level tapering toward a peak stays a future
BlockProgression concern. Pinned by StrengthAccessoryTests.
