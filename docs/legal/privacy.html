Gym Sync Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 14, 2026

Gym Sync is a workout app for training solo and with your crew. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what happens to it. The short version: your data exists to power your training and the social features you choose to use. We don't run ads, we don't sell your data, and we don't use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

1. What we collect

  • Account — you sign in with Apple. We receive the name and email Apple shares (you may use Apple's private email relay). You choose a username, an optional display name, and an optional avatar photo.
  • Training data — the workouts you log: exercises, sets, reps, weights, RPE, failures, rest times, routines, programs, personal records, and session history.
  • Body weight — if you log it. Your latest logged body weight is also stamped onto bodyweight-exercise sets so your training volume is honest.
  • Heart rate — if you connect a Bluetooth monitor or use the watch app during a session. During live group sessions, your live heart rate is shown to the crew you are training with, for the duration of the session.
  • Location — only while using the app, and only to confirm you're at a gym when you check in to a venue hub. We store the check-in (venue and time), not a movement trail. Location is never collected in the background.
  • Photos — pump-check photos you take or choose to share with friends, and any avatar images you upload.
  • Messages and social activity — crew chat messages and voice notes, friends, crew memberships, venue hub memberships, and any reports or blocks you file.

2. Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, Gym Sync writes your completed workouts to Apple Health and reads your heart rate during sessions. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, is never sold, and is never shared with third parties. The watch app reads heart rate only during an active session and saves nothing to Apple Health itself.

3. How we use your data

  • To run the app: logging, records, weight suggestions, progress charts, streaks, and scheduling.
  • To power the social features you opt into: crews, friends, feeds, and venue hubs.
  • To keep the community safe: processing reports and blocks.

We do not sell personal data, share it with data brokers, or use it for advertising. There are no third-party analytics or ad SDKs in the app.

4. What other people can see

  • Friends see what you share with them (pump checks, feed activity).
  • Crews you join see your sets, live progress, and heart rate during shared sessions, plus chat.
  • Venue hubs show your presence only if you turn on hub visibility, and hub features require an 18+ self-attestation. Solo workouts have their own privacy toggle.
  • Exercise demo videos play through YouTube's official embedded player; when you play one, YouTube's own privacy policy applies to that playback.

5. Storage and security

Your data is stored with Supabase (our hosting provider), protected in transit with TLS and encrypted at rest. Access is controlled with row-level security so features only ever read the data their rules allow.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in You → Settings → Delete Account, which permanently removes your account and its data from our systems. You can also revoke any device permission (location, camera, Bluetooth, Health, calendar) in iOS Settings at any time.

7. Children

Gym Sync is not directed at children under 13, and venue hub features require users to attest they are 18 or older.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the effective date above and note the change in the app.
