You are a strength-coach analyst. This pass exists to answer ONE blocking
question for our generator, and it should be answered precisely or not at all.

## The decision this feeds

Our generator can currently stack ~17 direct chest sets into a single
bro-split session. We want to cap per-session per-muscle volume — but two
evidence-backed numbers appear to disagree:

- the corpus's only STRONG weekly row: a systematic review binned volume as
  low (<12), moderate (12-20), high (>20) weekly sets, and moderate clearly
  beat low
- an 8-10 per-session ceiling cited from the volume landmarks

A naive session cap drops weekly volume BELOW the moderate band, trading a
stacking defect for a floor defect the strong row says is worse. So:

## Extract findings on

1. IS THERE A PER-SESSION CEILING distinct from the weekly one? What happens
   to the 10th, 15th, 20th set for one muscle in one session? Quote any
   number, and say plainly if the "junk volume" idea is contested.
2. DOES THE OPTIMUM DIFFER BY MUSCLE? Do calves, side delts, rear delts,

   chest, back, quads? Numbers per muscle wherever stated.
3. SMALL VS LARGE MUSCLE RECOVERY — is there a real difference in recovery
   rate, and does it license different frequency or per-session volume?
4. HOW FREQUENCY INTERACTS — if a per-session ceiling exists, does splitting
   the same weekly volume across more sessions actually produce more growth,
   or is weekly volume the thing that matters?
5. INDIVIDUAL VARIATION — how much does the optimum vary between people, and
   is a single fixed number defensible for an app at all?
6. THE HONEST BOUNDARY — where does the evidence run out? If the truthful
   answer is "weekly volume is what matters and per-session ceilings are
   coach heuristic", say so. That is the most useful finding we could get,
   because it decides whether we cap at all.
