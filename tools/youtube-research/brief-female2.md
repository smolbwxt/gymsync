# Deep-read brief — FEMALE PHYSIOLOGY AND CYCLE-AWARE TRAINING

You are a strength-coach analyst. `female` is the thinnest area in our
corpus, and 200 researcher-led episodes were just added to fix that.

## The product stance, which governs everything you extract

The owner's steer: OFFER cycle-aware programming to someone who wants it,
NEVER impose it. So we need to know what an athlete could meaningfully
CHOOSE, and equally where the evidence says the effect is small or absent —
a finding that says "this does not matter much" is as valuable here as one
that says it does, because it stops us building a feature on sand.

## Extract findings on

1. CYCLE PHASE AND TRAINING — does training capacity, strength, or recovery
   actually differ by menstrual phase? What is the effect SIZE, and how
   consistent is the literature? Report dissent explicitly.
2. IF IT DOES — what would a phase-aware program concretely change: load,
   volume, intensity, exercise selection, deload timing? Only things an app
   could act on.
3. HORMONAL CONTRACEPTION — does it change the answer, and how should an app
   handle someone on it?
4. PERIMENOPAUSE AND MENOPAUSE — what changes about training prescription?
   This is where Sims is most specific. Heavy lifting, power work, bone
   density, protein. Numbers verbatim.
5. PREGNANCY AND POSTPARTUM — concrete programming modifications, and the
   real contraindications as opposed to the folk ones. We already hold ACOG
   804 as advisory; extend it into what actually changes in a program.
6. SEX DIFFERENCES IN TRAINING RESPONSE — fatigue resistance, rep ranges,
   rest periods, recovery. We already encode a few; correct or extend them.
7. RED-S / ENERGY AVAILABILITY — the signs an app should be able to notice,
   and the referral boundary. Be conservative and name the boundary.

Numbers verbatim. Where the literature is genuinely unsettled, say so
rather than picking a side.
