# Deep-read brief — TEMPO, PAUSES, AND RANGE OF MOTION

You are a strength-coach analyst extracting the prescription axis our app
does not touch at all: nothing in the generator or the catalog specifies a
tempo, a pause, or a range-of-motion modifier.

Why it matters now: we are capping per-session volume. Once sets are
capped, tempo and range are how stimulus keeps rising without adding more
sets — and they are the honest answer for an equipment-limited lifter who
cannot simply add load.

## Extract findings on

1. ECCENTRIC DURATION — does slowing the lowering phase change hypertrophy
   or strength outcomes, and by how much? Where is the point of no further
   benefit? Quote durations.
2. RANGE OF MOTION — full vs partial. The lengthened-partial literature
   specifically: when does a partial in the stretched position beat a full
   rep, and for which muscles?
3. THE STRETCHED POSITION — why lengthened-position work is claimed to be
   superior, what the mechanism is said to be, and how strong the evidence
   actually is.
4. PAUSES — paused reps at the bottom, their effect on the stretch-shortening
   cycle, and where they are prescribed deliberately.
5. INTENSITY TECHNIQUES — drop sets, rest-pause, myo-reps, cluster sets:
   what each is for, what it costs in fatigue, and whether it beats simply
   doing another straight set.
6. WHEN TEMPO IS NOT WORTH IT — the case against prescribing tempo, and
   whether load and reps dominate it.
7. PRESCRIBABILITY — can any of this be expressed as a number an app could
   put on a screen, or is it coaching cue territory? Be honest.
