# Deep-read brief — OLYMPIC LIFTING FOR A GENERAL STRENGTH APP

You are a strength-coach analyst. Our app has no Olympic-lift programming
at all, and this pass is deliberately scoped to what a GENERAL app can
responsibly use — NOT competitive weightlifting programming, and NOT meet
preparation, which the owner has parked.

## Extract findings on

1. WHO SHOULD DO THESE AT ALL — is there a defensible case for programming
   cleans and snatches for a general lifter or a field-sport athlete, and
   what is the argument against? Report both sides.
2. DERIVATIVES AT LOWER TECHNICAL COST — high pull, hang variations, jump
   shrug, push press. Which carry most of the benefit without the coaching
   burden of the full lift? This is the most actionable question for us.
3. TEACHING PROGRESSION — the order these are taught in, and the
   prerequisites (mobility, front rack, overhead position) that gate them.
4. LOADING AND VOLUME — set/rep/percentage ranges for power development
   specifically, and why they differ from hypertrophy or max-strength work.
   Numbers verbatim.
5. FATIGUE AND PLACEMENT — where in a session and week these belong, and
   what they cost in recovery relative to their benefit.
6. WHEN NOT TO — populations, ages, or goals where these should simply not
   be prescribed by an app without a coach present.

Be conservative. If the honest conclusion is that an app should not
auto-prescribe these, say so — that is a useful finding.
