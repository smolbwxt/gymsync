# Deep-read brief — PROGRAMMING AROUND AN EQUIPMENT CONSTRAINT

You are a strength-coach analyst extracting what an automatic coach should
do when the athlete does not have a full gym.

This is a live gap in our app. The consult asks what equipment the athlete
has, and that answer now reaches the generator — but all the generator does
with it is FILTER the catalog. Nothing tells it how to program *around* the
constraint. A dumbbell-only lifter gets the barbell exercises removed and
nothing put back in their place.

Note before you start: this corpus is thin here. Our channels are gym-
equipped lifting-science channels, and only ~25 transcripts mention home or
minimal-equipment training at any depth. Report what is genuinely there and
say plainly when a question is unanswered. Do NOT pad.

## Extract findings on

1. PATTERN COVERAGE without a barbell — which movement patterns become hard
   or impossible, and what genuinely substitutes for each. Especially the
   hinge, which is the usual casualty.
2. LOADING without heavy implements — how to keep progressing when the
   dumbbells run out: reps, tempo, unilateral work, ROM, density, pauses.
   Numbers where stated.
3. EQUIVALENCE — where bands, bodyweight, machines and free weights produce
   comparable outcomes, and where they do NOT. Resistance profiles matter.
4. MINIMUM EFFECTIVE KIT — what actually needs buying first, and what is
   optional, if any source ranks it.
5. WHEN A CONSTRAINT CHANGES THE GOAL — is any goal (max strength, power)
   genuinely out of reach on limited kit, and what should be offered instead?

Report each finding with the confidence the source warrants and quote
numbers verbatim.
