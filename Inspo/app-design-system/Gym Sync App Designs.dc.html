The main event

Live Session — real-time rotation

The marquee flow: friends drop into a shared session and take turns on the lift, like sharing a bench. On your turn your heart rate goes live, you log reps · weight · RPE, and the crew hypes you up over voice and the soundbar. Two directions for the live screen — 2A Spotlight (you're the focus) and 2B Roster (the whole room at a glance) — bookended by the lobby and the recap.
